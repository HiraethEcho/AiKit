Yifei Chen, Jihao Liu, and Yanze Wang Flop between algebraically integrable foliations
We prove that for any two minimal models of an lc algebraically integrable foliated triple on potentially klt varieties, there exist small birational models that are connected by a sequence of flops. In particular, any two minimal models of lc algebraically integrable foliated triples on $$-factorial klt varieties are connected by a sequence of flops. We also discuss the connection between minimal models for possibly non-algebraically integrable foliations on threefolds, assuming the minimal model program for generalized foliated quadruples.
# Introduction
We work over the field of complex numbers $ C$.
between minimal models of varieties. Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety with a pseudo-effective canonical divisor $K_X$. The minimal model program theory predicts that after a $K_X$-MMP, i.e., a sequence of $K_X$-divisorial contractions and flips, one obtains a minimal model $X_$ of $X$, such that $X_$ has $$-factorial terminal singularities and $K_X_$ is nef, i.e., $K_X_ C 0$ for any curve $C$ on $X_$. However, it is well known that for different $K_X$-MMPs, the minimal model $X_$ may not be unique. Therefore, it is interesting to ask what the connection is between different minimal models of $X$.
Following the proof of the existence of the minimal model program for klt varieties [BCHM10], Kawamata proved that the induced birational map $X_1 X_2$ between any two minimal models $X_1$ and $X_2$ of a $$-factorial terminal projective variety $X$ can be decomposed into a sequence of $K_X_1$-flops [Theorem 1]Kaw08. The same lines of the proof also hold when $X$ is $$-factorial klt. Later, following the proof of the existence of the minimal model program for lc varieties [Bir12, HX13, Has19], Hashizume improved this result to the case when $X$ is $$-factorial lc [Has20], and also considered the connection between minimal models of $X$ when $X$ is not $$-factorial. More precisely, [Theorem 1.1]Has20 proved that for any lc projective variety $X$ and two $K_X$-MMPs $X X_1$, $X X_2$, such that $X_1$ and $X_2$ are minimal models of $X$, there exist small birational morphisms $X_1' arrow X_1$, $X_2' arrow X_2$, and the induced birational map $X_1' X_2'$ can be decomposed into a sequence of $K_X_1'$-flops. Hashizume's result was later further improved to the case of NQC lc generalized pairs [Cha24], based on the proof of the existence of the minimal model program for such structures [HL23, LX23a, LX23b, Xie22].

First, we briefly recall Kawamata's original proof [Theorem 1]Kaw08 on the flop connection between $$-factorial terminal pairs $(X_1,B_1)$ and $(X_2,B_2)$ associated with the birational map $: X_1 X_2$: we take a general ample divisor $L_2 0$ on $X_2$, consider its strict transform $L_1$ on $X_1$, and run a $(K_X_1+B_1+tL_1)$-MMP with scaling of an ample divisor for some $0<t 1$. This MMP terminates with $X_2$ and is a sequence of $(K_X_1+B_1)$-flops due to three reasons:
- Since $0<t 1$ and $(X_1,B_1)$ is klt, $(X_1,B_1+tL_1)$ is still klt.
- The bounded length of extremal rays ensures that this MMP is $(K_X_1+B_1)$-trivial.
- Since $L_1$ is big, this MMP terminates with a good minimal model by [BCHM10].
We want to follow the idea of [Theorem 1]Kaw08 to prove Theorem [thm: main triple]. With notations as in Theorem [thm: main triple] and considering (1-3) above, we encounter the following three difficulties:
- [(i)] Due to the failure of Bertini-type theorems, it is possible that $(X_2,_2,B_2+tL_2)$ is not lc for any ample $$-divisor $L_2 0$ and any $t>0$. - [(ii)] Even if $(X_2,_2,B_2+tL_2)$ is lc, we also do not know how to prove that $(X_1,_1,B_1+tL_1)$ is lc for any $0<t 1$. - [(iii)] Even if $(X_1,_1,B_1+tL_1)$ is lc, $L_1$ is only big. Unlike the usual klt pair case, we still do not know whether a $(K__1+B_1+tL_1)$-MMP with scaling of an ample divisor will terminate with a good minimal model in general.
Of course, the lengths of extremal rays are still bounded, at least for algebraically integrable foliations [Theorem 3.9]ACSS21, [Theorem 2.2.1]CHLX23.
For issue (i), the natural idea is to consider the generalized foliated quadruple $(X_2,_2,B_2;t:=t_2)$ instead of $(X_2,_2,B_2+tL_2)$, which is a standard way to bypass Bertini-type theorems since [LLM23]. For issue (iii), a key observation is that $K__2+B_2+tL_2$ is ample. Since $: X_1 X_2$ neither extracts nor contracts any divisors, $(X_2,_2,B_2,t)$ is a good minimal model of $(X_1,_1,B_1,t)$. Now, [Theorem 1.11]LMX24b ensures the termination of the $(K__1+B_1+t_X_1)$-MMP with scaling of ample divisors, provided that $(X_1,_1,B_1,t)$ is lc. Therefore, we are only left to resolve issue (ii); more precisely, we only need to show that $(X_1,_1,B_1,t)$ is lc for some $t>0$.
The difficulty in issue (ii) arises from the fact that $(X_2,_2,B_2)$ has lc centers, and any small perturbation along the generic point of any lc center may destroy the lc structure. A similar difficulty arose in [Has20] when considering flops connecting lc but not klt pairs $(X_1,B_1)$ and $(X_2,B_2)$. One key idea in [Proof of Theorem 1.1]Has20 is the observation that, over the generic point of any lc center of $(X_1,B_1)$ or $(X_2,B_2)$, the induced birational maps $X_1 X_2$ and $X_2 X_1$ are isomorphisms. In other words, there exist large open subsets $X^0_1 X_1$ and $X^0_2 X_2$ which contain all lc centers of $(X_1,B_1)$ and $(X_2,B_2)$, respectively, and the induced birational map $X^0_1 X^0_2$ is an isomorphism. In fact, [Section 4]Has20 provided counterexamples when no such large open subsets $X^0_1$ and $X^0_2$ exist, i.e., when the minimal models are not obtained as the outputs of the MMPs.
Unfortunately, the arguments in [Proof of Theorem 1.1]Has20 also do not work in our setting. This is because the lc centers of usual pairs form a proper closed subset of the ambient variety, but for any algebraically integrable foliation $ T_X$, the lc centers of $$ cover the entire variety $X$. Nevertheless, we have a way to resolve this issue: instead of only considering $(X_1,_1,B_1)$ and $(X_2,_2,B_2)$ and finding the corresponding open subsets $X^0_1$ and $X^0_2$, we aim to more concretely use the fact that $(X_1,_1,B_1)$ and $(X_2,_2,B_2)$ are outputs of MMPs.
More precisely, let $_i: (X,,B) (X_i,_i,B_i)$ be $(K_+B)$-MMPs. Then we may take a high foliated log resolution $X'arrow X$ of $(X,,B)$ so that the induced birational morphisms $X'arrow X_i$ are also foliated log resolutions of $(X_i,_i,B_i)$. The MMPs $X X_i$ lift to MMPs $X' X_i'$ with induced $$-factorial ACSS modifications $(X_i',_i,B_i')arrow (X_i,_i,B_i)$. Here are the key observations: - $X_2'arrow X_2$ extracts only finitely many divisors $E_j$ that are lc places of $(X_2,_2,B_2)$. We may choose $L_2$ so that it does not contain the generic point of the centers of any $E_j$.
- $E_j$ are also the divisors extracted by $X_1'arrow X_1$, and by a simple discrepancy comparison, $$ is an isomorphism near the generic point of any $_X_1E_j$. Thus, $L_1$ does not contain the generic point of any $_X_1E_j$. This implies that $_X_1'$ is the pullback of $L_1$.
Since $(X',',B',t)$ is lc for any $t>0$, $(X_1',_1',B_1',t)$ is lc for any $0<t 1$. Since $_X_1'$ is the pullback of $L_1$, $(X_1,_1,B_1,t)$ is lc. ## Final remarks
In our main theorems, we require that $X$ is $$-factorial klt or potentially klt. This is because we only know the existence of the minimal model program for algebraically integrable foliations under these extra conditions [Theorems 1.2, 1.3]LMX24b, [Theorem 1.11]CHLMSSX24. We expect the ``potentially klt" condition to be removed if the existence of the minimal model program is proven for any lc algebraically integrable foliated triples.
It seems that we still do not know whether flops will connect minimal models for non-NQC lc generalized pairs, even when the ambient variety is $$-factorial klt. For non-NQC $$-factorial klt generalized pairs, although we cannot find a written proof, a positive answer can be deduced following the same lines of the proof in [Proof of Theorem 1]Kaw08.
The dual question to ``flops connecting minimal models" is the Sarkisov program, which provides a way to connect Mori fiber spaces. The Sarkisov program was established in [Theorem 1.3]HM13 for $$-factorial klt pairs, in [Theorem 1.5]Liu21 for $$-factorial klt generalized pairs, and in [Theorem 1.1]Mas24 for $$-factorial F-dlt foliated triples $(X,,B)/U$ with $ X=3$, $=2$, and $ B=0$.
With the ``flop connection part" settled in this paper, it is interesting to ask whether we can establish the Sarkisov program for lc algebraically integrable foliated triples on $$-factorial klt varieties. This should follow from the log geography of minimal models, which is yet to be established for algebraically integrable foliations.
