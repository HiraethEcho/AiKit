# Theory Notes

This skill is based on the idea that high-quality translation is rewriting. The translator should be faithful to the source meaning, logic, tone, and context rather than to the surface order of source words.

## Translation As Rewriting

Siguo's view that "translation is rewriting" means the translator must recreate the source thought in idiomatic Chinese. Yu Kwang-chung similarly treats translation as limited creation: the translator needs a writer's pen, not a dictionary-matching reflex.

The practical consequence is simple: literal correspondence is useful only when it produces natural Chinese. When literal translation creates translationese, the translator should abandon the source form and preserve the source meaning through Chinese expression.

## Fidelity To Meaning

Real fidelity points to meaning, not wording. A word-for-word rendering can look precise while distorting the reading experience and argument structure. Good translation preserves the intellectual content while changing sentence order, voice, rhythm, and phrasing as needed.

## English Hypotaxis And Chinese Parataxis

English often relies on hypotaxis: relative clauses, conjunctions, prepositions, and nested sentence structures. Chinese often relies on parataxis: meaning, sequence, and context. This difference justifies splitting long English sentences, reordering information, and using shorter Chinese clauses.

Typical conversions:

- Background before conclusion.
- Cause before result.
- Known information before new information.
- Heavy explanation in separate sentences rather than overloaded noun phrases.

## Anti-Translationese Editing

Common symptoms of Europeanized Chinese include:

- overuse of 被
- long 的-chains
- mechanical 当……的时候
- 关于 / 有关……的问题
- unnecessary pronouns and possessives
- abstract noun stacks
- English-style sentence order
- stiff phrases such as 进行分析 when 分析 is enough

The reviewer role exists to catch these traces before final rewriting.

## Why Three Roles

The three-role workflow separates creative rewriting from critical review and final editing:

1. The lead translator focuses on meaning and fluency.
2. The reviewer applies pressure and finds residual translationese.
3. The final editor produces a clean publishable version.

This separation reduces the common AI failure mode where the model "polishes" a draft without escaping its English-shaped structure.
